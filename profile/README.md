---

## Arcflow Protocol

An AI game guide that lives inside your game. Arcflow is a lightweight PC overlay that uses computer vision to understand what's on screen, then answers on demand with puzzle solutions, collectible locations, tactics and lore, so you don't have to pause or alt-tab to a wiki.

- **Context-aware:** reads your current scene and situation to tailor its answers
- **On demand:** speaks only when asked, through the overlay or a hotkey
- **Light on resources:** asynchronous processing keeps it out of your frame rate
- **Single-player by design:** it doesn't inject code into your game

Arcflow is currently in **alpha** and supports a growing library of single-player titles. The current list is on the [website](https://arcflowprotocol.com).
